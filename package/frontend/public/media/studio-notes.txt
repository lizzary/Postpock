给月亮的回信 / A Letter to the Moon
海边夜行手记 · 第四章 / Coastal field notes · chapter four

潮水退得很远。月光散在水面上，像一封没有折好的信。
配色从靛蓝、浅紫到水边一点暖桃色。保留潮声里的空拍。

The tide pulled back a long way. Moonlight lay across the water like an unfolded letter.
The colors move from indigo through pale violet to a little warm peach at the waterline.
Leave a quiet beat between the waves.
